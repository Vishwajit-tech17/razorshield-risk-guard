RAZORSHIELD AI - CHARGEBACK POLICY KNOWLEDGE BASE

POLICY 1: UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTION

An unauthorized transaction occurs when a customer claims that they did not
make or authorize a payment.

Useful evidence includes:
- Payment authentication status
- Customer account information
- Device information
- IP country
- Previous successful transactions
- Transaction history
- Delivery information

If strong authentication was successfully completed, this may support the
merchant's response to an unauthorized transaction claim.


POLICY 2: CARD PAYMENT AUTHENTICATION

Authentication information can be used as supporting evidence during a
chargeback investigation.

A successful authentication result can indicate that additional verification
was completed during the payment process.

Authentication evidence should be included when responding to an applicable
chargeback.


POLICY 3: DELIVERY EVIDENCE

Delivery information can help establish that goods or services were provided.

Useful delivery evidence includes:
- Delivery status
- Delivery confirmation
- Shipping information
- Order information
- Date of delivery

A delivered order can provide supporting evidence against certain
chargeback claims.


POLICY 4: CUSTOMER TRANSACTION HISTORY

Previous successful transactions can provide useful context during an
investigation.

Relevant information includes:
- Number of previous transactions
- Successful transactions
- Previous chargebacks
- Previous refunds
- Account age

A long-standing customer with previous successful transactions may provide
different risk context from a newly created account.


POLICY 5: REFUND INFORMATION

Refund history should be considered during chargeback investigation.

Useful evidence includes:
- Previous refunds
- Refund status
- Refund dates
- Refund amount

If a refund has already been issued, this information should be considered
when investigating a related chargeback.


POLICY 6: NEW CUSTOMER RISK

Recently created accounts may require additional investigation.

An account with a short account age combined with unusual transaction
characteristics may represent increased risk.

Account age should not be used as the only reason for rejecting or escalating
a transaction.


POLICY 7: CHARGEBACK HISTORY

Previous chargebacks are an important risk indicator.

A customer with multiple previous chargebacks may require additional review.

Chargeback history should be considered together with other transaction and
customer-level evidence rather than being used as the sole decision factor.


POLICY 8: HUMAN REVIEW

High-risk cases should be available for human review.

Automated risk scoring should support investigators rather than blindly
automating decisions.

Cases with insufficient evidence, conflicting signals, or high financial
impact should be escalated for human investigation.


POLICY 9: EVIDENCE QUALITY

Evidence should be relevant to the chargeback reason and supported by
available transaction information.

The system should not invent evidence.

If required information is unavailable, the system should clearly indicate
that the evidence is unavailable.


POLICY 10: INVESTIGATION PRINCIPLE

RazorShield AI should combine:
- ML risk score
- Transaction information
- Customer history
- Authentication information
- Delivery information
- Refund information
- Relevant policy documents

The final investigation should provide evidence-based reasoning and clearly
distinguish between available evidence and missing evidence.
